# Interactive Learning Recommendations

## Scope and approach

This is an internal, one-time review of the English lesson PDFs in
`subjects-content/`. It informs potential platform interactives; it does not
copy, import, publish, or seed textbook content. Each row is grounded in its
corresponding lesson and names a deliberately focused MVP rather than a static
content page.

Shared delivery requirements: localize all student-facing copy in Arabic and
English; preserve RTL layout; support touch, keyboard, and screen readers; do
not rely on colour alone; and offer reduced-motion/text-equivalent versions of
animated states. Use fictional data and scenarios, particularly for privacy,
security, and legal topics.
